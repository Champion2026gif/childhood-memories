01 — About

Who I am

I'm an NCV IT graduate from False Bay College, with a solid base in programming, networking, hardware, and multimedia. Over three years of study, I've moved from the basics of computer assembly right up to building small software systems and designing databases. I like understanding how something works underneath, not just how to use it.

Most of what I know comes from hands-on projects: writing a small web application in JavaScript, setting up and securing a wireless network, assembling a computer from raw parts, and mapping out a database with UML diagrams before building it in SQL. I've also worked with graphics, video, and audio editing, so I can bring a creative side to a technical problem when needed.

What drives me is problem-solving — finding the reason something is broken and fixing it properly. I want to keep learning and grow into a role where I can apply IT skills to real challenges, whether that's in networking, software development, or support. This page will grow as I take on more projects.
