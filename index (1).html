Hobbies

Drawing

I used to spend hours filling sketchbooks with drawings of animals and imaginary creatures.

I remember once drawing an entire zoo map on a single piece of paper — every animal had its own enclosure and a little sign.

Riding My Bike

Every afternoon after school, I would race my bike around the neighbourhood until it got dark.

I once attempted a daring jump over a ramp my friend built from bricks and a plank of wood — it did not go as planned.

Toys

LEGO

I could spend an entire weekend building elaborate LEGO cities complete with roads, houses, and tiny people.

My proudest creation was a massive spaceship so large it took up half the living room floor, much to my parents' frustration.

Yo-Yo

I practised yo-yo tricks relentlessly until I could do "Walk the Dog" without even looking down.

I once entered a playground competition and came second — the winner could do tricks I still don't understand to this day.
